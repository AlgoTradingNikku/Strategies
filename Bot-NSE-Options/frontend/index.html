Scanner & Option Grid Settings

Risk Management & Guardrails

These settings govern the pre-trade risk gates. All new auto-orders must pass every enabled check. Values are also editable in config.yml.

Signal Quality & Scoring

Advanced pre-trade filters that reject low-probability signals BEFORE Sprint-1 risk gates. Also drives the transparent 0-100 signal score you see on every scan row.

Dynamic Position Sizing

Replaces static lot count with risk-based sizing. Every trade risks a controlled fraction of equity so a stop-loss hit costs exactly risk_per_trade_pct of your account. Quantities always snap DOWN to lot-size (75 for NIFTY, 30 for BANKNIFTY).

Alpha Enhancers

Market-context filters: VIX regime, session weighting, POC distance, greeks. All fail-open on missing data.
